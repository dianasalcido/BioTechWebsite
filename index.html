Why CSB matters

Advanced biotechnology belongs here.

The Central Valley is an agricultural powerhouse. The Centre for Sustainable Biotechnology helps connect that strength to new scientific possibilities, sustainable solutions, and regional career pathways.

We make biotechnology more approachable, more practical, and more connected to the people and places it can serve.
